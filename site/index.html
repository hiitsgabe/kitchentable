A card table for friends who are somewhere else.

You bring the cards. The app brings the table, the shuffler, the dice and the connection. Nobody runs a server, nobody makes an account, and nothing is sold.

Play in your browser Get it onGoogle Play

Any card game

Piles of cards with state on them: move, tap, flip, counters, tokens, dice. Enough for Magic and Pokemon.

A room is a code

Send a link, hold up a QR, or read seven characters out loud. Friends join from any phone or browser.

Voice and chat

Talk while you play. Voice goes phone to phone; the game travels over public relays.

Your cards, your device

Ships empty. Nothing is downloaded until you switch a card source on in Settings.
